Python is powering breakthrough exascale scientific discoveries -- in this
interactive tutorial attendees learned how to write, debug, profile, and
optimize high-performance, multi-node GPU applications in Python: CuPy for
drop-in GPU acceleration of NumPy workflows, Numba for writing custom
kernels that match the performance of C++ and Fortran, and mpi4py for
scaling across thousands of nodes. Along the way we profiled our code,
debugged tricky kernels, and leveraged foundational and domain-specific
accelerated libraries. Everything was hands-on: short and interactive
lectures by expert instructors were paired with guided Jupyter notebooks
that introduced each concept and had attendees immediately apply it in
bite-sized exercises (shallow water equation, SVD image compression,
Mandelbrot, etc).
